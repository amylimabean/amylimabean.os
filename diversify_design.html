A Note from the Founder

Diversify Design is a community I founded that supports designers from historically underrepresented backgrounds. It aims to provide a safe and transparent space for underrepresented folx in the design industry connect, learn, and grow together through meetups and knowledge exchanges.

I've hosted events that have ranged from a 500+ person meetup at Config to an intimate gathering of women in design for Womens History Month with the same core mission: empowering marginalized voices in our industry.

I'll keep it real tho, fam: running a community is hard work. Over the past year, I've put a pause on programming to focus on personal matters: navigating a company-wide layoff, starting a new job, and prioritizing my mental health.

But! I have some exciting ideas in the works, coming soon to a city near you
